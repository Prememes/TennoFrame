Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026

TennoFrame ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website tennoframe.com and the TennoFrame mobile app (collectively, the "Service"). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use the Service:

  • Account information — If you choose to sign in, we collect your name and email address via Google or email/password authentication (powered by Firebase Authentication).
  • User content — Reviews and build submissions you post are stored in our database (Firestore) and associated with your display name.
  • Usage data — Standard server logs including your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected automatically when you use the Service.
  • Device identifiers — On the mobile app, an advertising identifier may be used to serve personalized ads (see Advertising section below).

How We Use Your Information

  • To display your name alongside reviews and builds you submit
  • To maintain and improve the Service
  • To serve advertisements that help keep the Service free
  • To communicate with you if you contact us

Advertising

We use Google AdSense on the website and Google AdMob in the mobile app to display advertisements. These services may use cookies and device identifiers to show you personalized ads based on your interests.

On iOS, we request App Tracking Transparency permission before using your device's advertising identifier. You can opt out at any time in your device Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

To learn more about how Google uses data, visit policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Data Storage

User accounts and reviews are stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore), located in the United States. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Third-Party Services

The Service uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database
  • Google AdSense / AdMob — advertising
  • Digital Extremes (Warframe API) — game data source

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. You may also delete your account directly from the app, which removes your stored reviews and profile information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
